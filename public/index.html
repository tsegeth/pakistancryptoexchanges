Three Questions Before You Open A PKR Account

There is exactly one way rupees reach a crypto exchange in Pakistan today, and knowing it changes which venue you pick.

Is Crypto Legal In Pakistan?

Yes, and for individuals it always was. The 2018 State Bank circular everyone calls "the ban" told banks to stay away from virtual assets. It never made holding or trading crypto a crime for you.

Parliament passed the Virtual Assets Act, 2026, after the Senate cleared it on 27 February and the National Assembly on 3 March. On 14 April 2026 the State Bank issued BPRD Circular Letter No. 10 of 2026, which replaced the 2018 instruction and lets banks open accounts for PVARA-licensed providers, including segregated client money accounts in rupees. Banks still cannot buy, hold, or trade crypto with their own money or yours.

Checked 2026. Sources: CoinDesk, ProPakistani.

Which Exchange Has A PVARA License?

None of them. Anyone telling you their exchange is "PVARA licensed" is selling you something.

On 12 December 2025 PVARA gave No Objection Certificates to Binance and HTX. Chairman Bilal bin Saqib said on the record that these are not blanket approvals: they let a firm register with the Financial Monitoring Unit, set up a local subsidiary, and then apply for a license. PVARA published its final regulations on 21 August 2026 and opened the licensing portal the next day, with a 5 September 2026 cutoff for anyone already operating. About 70 companies filed, and every one of them is still in the queue.

Checked 2026. Sources: Dawn, PVARA, TechJuice.

How Do Rupees Get Into An Exchange?

Peer to peer, and only peer to peer. No global exchange takes a direct PKR card payment or a wire from your bank today. You buy from another person, the exchange holds the coin in escrow, and you pay them over Raast, a bank transfer, Easypaisa, or JazzCash.

Raast is the State Bank's own instant rail: free for customers, credited within 20 seconds by regulation, and it moved 664 million person-to-person payments worth PKR 18.9 trillion in the first quarter of 2026. Most P2P trades ride it, which also means your bank sees every transfer. That is the part worth reading twice in the red flags.

Checked 2026. Source: State Bank of Pakistan.

How We Check Every Crypto Exchange In Pakistan

Most "best exchange in Pakistan" pages are affiliate links with numbers copied from each other. This is the whole method, so you can audit ours. If a claim here has no source you can open, treat it as wrong and email us.

PVARA Standing

The heaviest input, because it decides whether an exchange can legally keep serving you. A No Objection Certificate confirmed by PVARA scores 90. A license application reported by credible press but absent from any published register scores 60 to 65. No known filing scores 20. Nothing scores 100, because no exchange holds a full PVARA license yet and we will not hand out a top mark for paperwork in progress.

PKR Funding

Can you get rupees in and out without leaving the exchange? A working peer-to-peer market with Pakistani payment methods and visible depth scores high. A venue you can only reach by bridging stablecoins in from somewhere else scores low, because that hop costs money and adds a failure point. Where we could not confirm a route ourselves, the cell reads unverified.

Cost

Standard non-VIP spot maker and taker fees from the exchange's own fee page or help center, before token discounts. We note the token discount separately, because it means holding an asset that can fall. This is the least important criterion here: the gap between the cheapest and the dearest venue is about 0.10 percent, and one bad P2P fill or one frozen withdrawal wipes out a year of that difference.

User Signal

Trustpilot score and review count, plus the one-star share and the themes inside it. Trustpilot is a complaint channel, not a representative survey, so scores across this category are grim. What matters is the comparison between exchanges and the pattern in the complaints. "Support was slow" is noise. "Withdrawals blocked under unexplained risk control", repeated hundreds of times, is a signal. Two flags count as serious on their own: a rating withheld for a guidelines breach, which follows fake-review removal, and an unclaimed company profile.

Red Flags On A Pakistani P2P Crypto Trade

Every item here has cost someone in this market real rupees, and none of them take more than a minute to check.

  • The Buyer Pays From Someone Else's Account

    The name on the transfer has to match the name on the exchange account. A mismatched sender is how stolen funds end up in your bank statement, and your bank will act on the complaint, not on your explanation.

  • Someone Wants To Trade Off The Platform

    Outside escrow you have no dispute channel at all. "Let's do it on WhatsApp, the fee is lower" is the most common way people here get cleaned out.

  • You Are Writing "Crypto" In The Transfer Note

    Banks flag it. In December 2021 the FIA froze 1,064 bank accounts over crypto-linked transactions and banks blocked the cards attached to them. The law has moved since. The freeze reflex at the bank has not.

  • The Price Is Far Off The Market

    An offer several percent better than every other ad on the book is bait. Price the trade against at least two venues before you accept it.

  • You Are Releasing Coin On A Screenshot

    Screenshots are trivial to fake. Release only after the money shows in your account balance and you have refreshed it yourself. Not on a notification, not on an image.

  • A Platform Claims It Is "PVARA Licensed"

    Nobody is, as of the 2026 check. Two firms hold No Objection Certificates and about seventy have applications in. A finished license does not exist yet, so the claim is either careless or a lie.

Crypto Tax In Pakistan: What You Owe And What Is Still A Proposal

Most pages on this subject get it wrong. Pakistan has not enacted a crypto-specific capital gains rate. The widely copied "15 percent under the Finance Act 2025" line does not survive a look at primary sources.

Crypto-Specific Ratenone enacted

No dedicated rate is in force. Proposals for the 2026–27 budget ranged from 10–20 percent to 20–30 percent through a new section of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001. No final rate has been announced.

Trading Gains Todaytaxable

Taxed under the general rules of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001. Gains on disposal are capital gains, and the absence of a special rate is not an exemption.

Mining And Stakingbusiness income

Treated as income rather than capital gains and taxed at your slab rate. An FBR official put it plainly: Pakistan taxes income regardless of how it is earned.

Platform Reportingcoming

Licensed providers sit under PVARA supervision and Financial Monitoring Unit registration. Assume anything you do on a licensed venue is visible to the authorities.

Filingannual return

Crypto gains go in your normal income tax return. Confirm the current year's due date with FBR, because it moves and we do not publish dates we have not re-checked.
